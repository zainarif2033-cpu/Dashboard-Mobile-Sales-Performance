# Mobile Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Summary
This project presents an end-to-end Power BI Mobile Sales Analysis designed to evaluate sales trends, identify top-performing regions, and track revenue growth to drive data-backed business decisions.
This Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard using Excel and Power BI to evaluate sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, brand performance, and transaction. The project involved data preparation and analysis in Excel, followed by KPI development and interactive visualizations in Power BI. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, customers,transactions,payment methods, brands, and time based performance to support datadriven business analysis.

# Key Insights
## Top Brand: 
● Motorola recorded the highest total sales by brand at approximately 13.7M, followed closely by Apple at approximately 13.6M.
## Average Value:
● Apple recorded the highest Total Average value at 42,350, followed by Motorola at 41,148.
## Top Customer:
● Ahmed was the highest-value customer by total sales, generating approximately 8.88M in sales.
## Payment Methods:
● Credit Card was the most frequently used payment method, accounting for 86 transactions, followed by UPI with 80.
